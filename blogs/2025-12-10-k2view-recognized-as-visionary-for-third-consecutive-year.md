K2view Recognized as Visionary for Third Consecutive Year in 2025 Gartner® Magic Quadrant™ for Data Integration Tools PALO ALTO, CA – December 10, 2025 – K2view, a global leader in data management and AI-ready data solutions, today announced that Gartner, the world’s leading information technology research and advisory company, has once again positioned K2view as a Visionary in the 2025 Gartner Magic Quadrant for Data Integration Tools. The company significantly improved its Ability to Execute ratings and overall position in the Magic Quadrant compared to last year's report.
